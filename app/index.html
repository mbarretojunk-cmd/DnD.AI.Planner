Adventure log

How the game works
  • Each evening a monster shows up that takes 6 hits. At least 4 must be must-dos (cat basics first, then that day's scheduled chores, then dinner and dishes, then the daily tidy). The other 2 can be any task: an extra, a weekly chore, reading, or more must-dos. Kill it before midnight and nobody takes damage.
  • Hits past the 6 are bonus loot: their XP plus 5 gold each when the monster falls.
  • If the monster survives, each missing hit lets an open must-do strike the member who guards it for 6 damage: Food & home → the cleric, House duties → the fighter, Cat care → the familiar, Free time → the wizard, lunch prep and quests → the rogue. Habits set to specific weekdays count as daily on those days.
  • Weekly habits are checked on Sunday at midnight. Each missing session deals 6 damage.
  • Finished habits heal their guardian by 3 HP. Finishing Sunday lunch prep gives a long rest: everyone fully healed and back on their feet.
  • A member at 0 HP goes down. They get back up after two wins in a row.
  • If the whole party goes down, you lose a level and half your gold.
  • Levelling up fully heals the party and raises everyone's max HP.
  • Each member also levels up on their own from their kind of task (cat care grows the familiar, house duties the fighter, and so on). Every member level adds 3 max HP. Their personal log is on their card.
  • Sick or away? Take a rest day from the Tonight tab: no fight, no damage, no XP. You get two per month.
  • Working in the lab on a weekend? Play the lab wild card on that day: weekend chores, lunch prep and weekly targets are excused, only daily basics count, and you get a 15 XP overtime bonus. No limit.
